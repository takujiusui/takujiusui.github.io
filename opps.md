### Current openings

The Usui Lab will open its doors at Yale EEB in January 2027! We are currently looking for prospective **graduate students** (to start in September 2027; applications due December 1) and **postdoctoral researchers** (to start anytime from January 2027).

**Prospective graduate students:** If you are interested in joining my lab, please check out the [Yale EEB graduate program](https://eeb.yale.edu/academics/graduate-program) for details on how to formally apply. Students will need to [contact](mailto:usuitakuji@gmail.com) their prospective advisor before applying to the graduate program (the admission deadline is December 1).

**Prospective postdoctoral scholars:** We are searching for a postdoctoral scholar who is excited about eco-evolutionary dynamics in plant populations and communities and/or in plant-microbe communities. Research themes include, but are not limited to, how species interactions shape (and are shaped by) eco-evolutionary processes across space and in response to global change, how species interactions shape plant invasion dynamics, and how plant and plant-microbe communities assemble and evolve to coexist. The postdoctoral scholar will use duckweeds and/or the duckweed-microbe system as an experimental model in the lab and/or in the field, including, but not limited to lab microcosm, outdoor mesocosm, or field-based approaches. We are ideally looking for candidates with expertise in experimental approaches and who are excited to incorporate molecular and/or microbial approaches to ecological and evolutionary research. Please get in touch!
