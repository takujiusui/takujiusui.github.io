## Join us!

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students, postdoctoral scholars, and undergraduate students.

We are an inclusive and collaborative group motivated by curiosity and learning. We value creativity, curiosity, and respect when probing new and long-standing questions in evolutionary ecology.

If you are interested in joining my lab, please reach out to me [via email](mailto:usuitakuji@gmail.com) if you are interested. with your CV, and a few sentences on what excites and motivates you, and what about my lab interests you.

### Current openings

The Usui Lab will open its doors at Yale EEB in January 2027! We are currently looking for prospective **graduate students** (to start in September 2027; applications due December 1) and **postdoctoral researchers** (to start anytime from January 2027).

**Prospective graduate students:** If you are interested in joining my lab, please check out the [Yale EEB graduate program](https://eeb.yale.edu/academics/graduate-program) for details on how to formally apply. Students will need to [contact](mailto:usuitakuji@gmail.com) their prospective advisor before applying to the graduate program (the admission deadline is December 1).

**Prospective postdoctoral scholars:** We are searching for a postdoctoral scholar who is excited about eco-evolutionary dynamics in plant populations and communities, and/or in plant-microbe communities, using duckweeds as a model experimental system in the lab and/or in the field. Research themes include, but are not limited to, how evolution shapes and in shaped by species interactions and environmental variation, and how these processes unfold across space. We are looking for candidates with expertise in experimental approaches (in the lab, greenhouse, or field) and who are excited to incorporate molecular and/or microbial approaches to ecology. Please get in touch!
